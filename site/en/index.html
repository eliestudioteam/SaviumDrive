Your game saves, safe in your Google Drive

Savium Drive is a free, open-source Windows app that backs up your games' save files to your own Google Drive account. Never lose your progress when you reinstall Windows, switch computers or play a game without cloud saves.

Download for Windows View the code on GitHub

Windows 10 and 11 · English and Spanish · MIT license

Features

Everything you need to never lose a save again.

🎮

Library

All your games in one place, with their cover art. Search by name and sort by name or by last backup.

➕

Add a game

Type its name: Savium Drive finds its cover and the folder where the game keeps its saves.

☁️

Backups

Real-time progress. Updating a backup only uploads the files that changed, and you can cancel at any time.

🗂️

Versions

Several independent saves per game (V1, V2, V3…), each with its own description and history.

♻️

Restore

Bring back any version. If your local saves are newer, Savium Drive warns you before replacing them.

💻

Multiple computers

Sign in on another PC and your library is there. Each computer remembers its own save folder.

Screenshots

A simple interface, with light and dark themes. (The screenshots show the app in Spanish.)

Your privacy

Your saves are stored only in your Google Drive. Savium Drive can only see the files it creates, never the rest of your Drive. It has no servers of its own, no ads and no telemetry.
