Privacy Policy for Savium Drive

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Savium Drive is an open-source Windows desktop app made by Eliestudio. It backs up your games' save files to your own Google Drive account. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Summary

  • Your saves are stored only in your Google Drive. Savium Drive has no servers of its own, and nobody else (including Eliestudio) can see your files.
  • The app can only see the files it creates in your Drive, nothing else.
  • There is no telemetry, advertising or usage analytics, and your data is never sold or shared.

Your Google account data

To connect to Google Drive, Savium Drive asks for your permission through Google sign-in (OAuth). It only requests the drive.file scope: access to files and folders created by the app itself (the SaviumBackups folder and the savium_config.json file). It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

With that permission, the app:

  • creates, uploads, downloads, copies, renames and moves to the trash its own backups;
  • reads your account name and your available Drive storage, to show them in the app.

Savium Drive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your session is stored only on your computer, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SaviumDrive. Signing out from the app revokes the permission with Google and deletes that data. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account settings.

Data on your computer

To find your games and their saves, Savium Drive looks on your computer at the usual game and launcher folders (for example Steam or Epic Games) and at save-file locations. That information is only used inside the app and is never sent anywhere.

Your preferences (language, theme, added games…) are stored on your computer, in %APPDATA%\SaviumDrive.

Third-party services

Besides Google Drive, the app connects to these services. None of them receives your Google account data or your saves:

ServicePurposeWhat is sent
SteamGridDBShowing each game's cover artThe name of the game being searched
GitHub (api.github.com)Checking for and downloading Savium Drive updatesNothing personal: it only asks for the latest release
GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com)Downloading the public Ludusavi game list, which says where each game stores its savesNothing personal

As with any internet connection, these services may log your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Children

Savium Drive is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its new date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the notes of the corresponding release.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, open an issue on GitHub.
